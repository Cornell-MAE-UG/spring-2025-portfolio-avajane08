During summer 2026 I was an engineering intern at Rigidized Metals, a company that makes textured sheet metal. My work covered a few areas. I used an optical profilometer to scan patterned sheet metal samples and collect depth and surface area data for the company's catalog of pattern reports. I learned Blender well enough to build a custom textured roll from an AutoCAD sketch. I also redesigned and built a rig for deflection testing of textured samples, taking the drawings through laser cutting, bending and assembly.

Two documents came out of this work. The first is my summer report, which covers each project and the skills I developed. The second is a step-by-step guide I wrote for creating a custom textured roll in Blender.
