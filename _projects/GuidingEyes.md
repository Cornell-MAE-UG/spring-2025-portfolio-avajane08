I am a member of Cornell's chapter of Guiding Eyes for the Blind and have been a sitter since Fall 2025. As a sitter, I look after guide dog puppies twice a week during the school day. The dogs I sit are usually between 4 months and 1.5 years old.

My job is to reinforce the basic training the puppies are already learning and to help socialize them on a college campus. Spending time around classes, crowds, and new places helps them get comfortable in busy environments, which is an important part of preparing them to become guide dogs.
